# ACP-304: Persist policy audit trail

## Problem
Policy decisions need durable auditability for post-run review and governance.

## Scope
- Persist policy evaluation events and outcomes.
- Include rationale, rule identifiers, and linked actions.
- Link audit events to goals and runs.

## Out of Scope
- Centralized external compliance dashboards.
- Real-time policy analytics.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Policy decisions are queryable after reload.
- Audit events include enough context for human review.
- Audit records can be correlated with execution history.

## Dependencies
- ACP-301, ACP-302

## Validation
- Tests for policy audit persistence and correlation.
